Brighter than Blue

Brighter than Blue is an independent consultancy for engineering delivery leadership, drawing on more than 20 years of running delivery inside regulated and scaling organisations.

The work takes three shapes: delivery manager contracting for a specific programme or team; transformation and delivery consulting on DevOps, agile and flow, AI-assisted delivery and engineering metrics; and interim or fractional engineering leadership for a fixed period.

It suits organisations with a specific delivery problem and real scope to change how delivery works, and a balanced view of AI — used to improve how teams work, not to replace people or judgement. The approach is transparent by default: progress, risks and data in the open, and outcomes and user needs ahead of the numbers.
